Management System, mapping how the different modules (Logistics, Procurement, 
Inventory) interact, what buttons to click on each page, who is responsible, 
and where the next process must proceed.

STEP-BY-STEP USER MANUAL
================================================================================

STEP 1: ACCREDITING A SUPPLIER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Before generating any Purchase Orders (POs) for parts or maintenance services, 
you must have active, accredited suppliers in the database.

• Location: Procurement -> Suppliers
• Who: Procurement Officer / Administrator
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "Suppliers" page.
  2. Click the "Add Supplier" button (top right).
  3. Fill in the supplier details (Company Name, Contact Person, Email, Phone, 
     Address, Tax ID) and click "Save". 
     *Note: The supplier is created in the "Pending" status.*
  4. Find the supplier card in the list under the "Pending" tab.
  5. Click the "Verify" (or "Accredit") button on the card.
  6. The system sets the status to "Accredited" and registers a 1-year active 
     accreditation certificate.
• Next Process:
  The supplier is now ready to receive Purchase Orders. They will appear in the 
  supplier dropdown menus in Job Orders and Purchase Orders.


STEP 2: REQUESTING MAINTENANCE (PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE SYSTEM)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Buses are tracked by mileage. When a bus reaches its maintenance threshold or is 
overdue for servicing, a maintenance request must be submitted.

• Location: Logistics -> Predictive Maintenance System (PMS)
• Who: Fleet Manager / Mechanic / Service Adviser
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "Predictive Maintenance" page.
  2. The page dashboard displays buses in three categories:
     - Overdue Service (Red)
     - Upcoming Service (Yellow)
     - Healthy / Active (Green)
  3. Locate the bus that needs maintenance (e.g., in the Overdue list).
  4. Click the "Request WO" (Request Work Order) button next to the bus.
  5. In the pop-up modal:
     - Select the Priority (Low, Medium, or High).
     - Enter details of what needs to be checked or fixed in the "Notes" field.
  6. Click "Submit WO Request".
• Next Process:
  This API call immediately creates a new "Pending" Work Order in the database. 
  The process now moves to the Work Orders (W.O) page.


STEP 3: PROCESSING THE WORK ORDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The maintenance request from the PMS page must be officially approved and assigned 
a Job Order so mechanics can begin working.

• Location: Procurement -> W.O (Work Orders)
• Who: Head Mechanic / Logistics-in-charge / Super Admin
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "W.O" page.
  2. Look at the top of the "Pending" list to find the newly requested Work Order.
  3. Review the description and assigned bus.
  4. Click the "Approve" button. The status changes to "Approved".
  5. Once approved, a new button labeled "Generate Job Order" will appear.
  6. Click "Generate Job Order".
• Next Process:
  The system automatically generates a corresponding Job Order. The process 
  now moves to the Job Orders (J.O) page.


STEP 4: EXECUTING THE JOB ORDER (CONFIRMING WORK)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The mechanic must confirm they are starting the job to update the bus's status 
in the fleet registry.

• Location: Procurement -> J.O (Job Orders)
• Who: Mechanic / Service Adviser
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "J.O" page.
  2. Find the Job Order that was just created (status will be "Pending").
  3. Click "Confirm" (or "Start Job"). 
     *Note: This updates the Job Order status to "In Progress" and marks the 
     bus in the fleet registry as "Under Maintenance" so it cannot be 
     accidental booked for trips.*
• Next Process:
  - If parts or tools are needed to complete the work, proceed to STEP 5 (below).
  - If no parts are needed, proceed directly to STEP 6 (Complete Job).


STEP 5: ORDERING PARTS VIA PURCHASE ORDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If the maintenance work requires purchasing parts (e.g., new tires, oil, filters), 
a Purchase Order must be generated and approved.

• Location: Procurement -> J.O (Job Orders) -> "Generate PO" Modal
• Who: Mechanic / Procurement Officer
• Steps:
  1. Find the active Job Order on the "J.O" page.
  2. Click the "Generate P.O." button on the Job Order card.
  3. A modal opens:
     - Under "Supplier", select an accredited supplier from the dropdown. 
       *Note: Blacklisted or pending suppliers are filtered out.*
     - Under "Items", list the parts, quantity, and cost required.
  4. Click "Submit PO Request". This creates a "Draft" Purchase Order.
  5. Go to the "P.O" page (Procurement -> P.O).
  6. Select your draft PO and click "Submit for Verification".
  7. A Procurement Auditor reviews the PO and clicks "Verify".
  8. A Super Admin / EVP clicks "Approve".
• Next Process:
  Once the PO is approved, the parts are ordered and delivered. The mechanic 
  uses the parts to fix the bus. The process moves back to the Job Orders (J.O) page.


STEP 6: COMPLETING MAINTENANCE & CLOSING THE JOB ORDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Once the physical repair work is finished, the Job Order must be closed to 
release the bus back into active service.

• Location: Procurement -> J.O (Job Orders)
• Who: Head Mechanic / Service Adviser
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "J.O" page.
  2. Find the "In Progress" Job Order.
  3. Click the "Complete" button.
     *Note: This changes the Job Order status to "Completed". It also updates 
     the Work Order status to "Completed".*
  4. (Optional but recommended): Go to Logistics -> Fleet, select the bus, and 
     update its "Last Serviced Date" and "Next Service Due" based on the work done.
• Next Process:
  The bus is updated in the database as "Active / Ready". The safety lock is 
  now cleared, meaning this bus can now be assigned to new trip tickets.


STEP 7: BOOKING & APPROVING A TRIP TICKET
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Now that the bus is healthy and certified, it can be dispatched on a commercial trip.

• Location: Logistics -> Trip Ticket & Logistics -> Overview
• Who: Logistics Officer / Super Admin
• Steps:
  1. Go to the "Trip Ticket" page.
  2. Click "Create Trip Ticket" (or edit a pending one).
  3. Assign the Driver and the Bus (the bus we just repaired).
  4. Click "Create".
  5. On the Trip Ticket list, click "Approve".
     *Note: The backend safety gate checks if the assigned bus has any pending 
     or uncompleted maintenance. Because we successfully completed the JO/WO in 
     Step 6, this check passes instantly.*
  6. Go to Logistics -> Overview.
• Next Process:
  The trip ticket is now active. You will see the scheduled trip plotted on the 
  Overview Calendar, and it is marked as "Approved / Active".


================================================================================
KEY BUSINESS RULES & SAFETY GATE SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. ACCREDITED SUPPLIERS ONLY:
   - You cannot generate a Purchase Order (PO) to any supplier unless they are 
     under status = 'accredited'.
   - The dropdown menus on J.O (Generate PO) and P.O creation pages enforce this.
   - The backend Laravel controllers will reject any attempt to save a PO with 
     a non-accredited supplier.

2. BUS MAINTENANCE SAFETY SHIELD:
   - When a bus is marked as "Under Maintenance" (triggered when J.O goes 
     "In Progress"), it cannot be booked for active trips.
   - A trip ticket cannot be approved if the selected bus has an outstanding 
     unresolved maintenance record.

3. CROSS-PAGE UPDATE SYNC:
   - The Overview page calendar and the Trip Tickets list use a shared cache key 
     ('trip-tickets'). Clicking "Approve" or "Reject" on either screen immediately 
     syncs the calendars and tables across the system without requiring browser reload.
