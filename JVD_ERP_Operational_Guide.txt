2. CORE AUTOMATED MAINTENANCE & TRIP PIPELINES
================================================================================

A. THE MAINTENANCE & PROCUREMENT PIPELINE (DRIVER -> HM -> SA -> JO -> PO)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------
   This pipeline governs vehicle repairs, part acquisitions, and vendor invoices:
   
   1. [DRIVER W.O. REQUEST] -> A driver (e.g. Eduardo Deblios) notices a bus issue 
      and submits a Work Order request from their personalized driver dashboard. 
      The request enters `pending_verification` (stored as pending_approval).
   2. [HEAD MECHANIC VERIFICATION] -> Head Mechanic Arnold Navero reviews the 
      driver's request, inspects the bus, customizes/corrects parts details if 
      needed, and approves the request. This forwards the W.O. to the Service Adviser.
   3. [SERVICE ADVISER APPROVAL GATE] -> Service Adviser Minda Hernandez reviews 
      the verified W.O. or overrides/creates a new one directly. Upon approval, she 
      clicks "Approve", which transitions the status to "open" and automatically 
      generates a customizable Job Order (J.O.).
   4. [JOB ORDER BRANCHING (PURCHASE VS. LABOR-ONLY)]:
      - [PURCHASE REQUIRED]: If the J.O. requires spare parts from an external vendor, 
        Purchasing Manager Jaymart Ogoy generates an itemized Purchase Order (P.O.) 
        consigned to an Accredited Supplier.
      - [PURCHASE NOT REQUIRED]: If the repair is labor-only or utilizes in-house 
        spares, the J.O. bypasses the P.O. stage and is completed by the mechanics.
   5. [PMS UPDATE] -> Upon J.O. completion, the PMS service automatically resets the 
      bus service mileage and restores the bus to "available" status.

B. THE TRIP OPERATIONS & REVENUE PIPELINE (TRIP -> DISPATCH -> COMMISSION -> COLLECTION)
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   This pipeline links customer booking, logistical scheduling, and revenue collection:
   
   1. [TRIP TICKET CREATION] -> Generated under Operations by Reservation Officer 
      Mia Abella or Corporate Secretary Crisalyn Cahalhal.
   2. [FLEET & DRIVER ALLOCATION] -> Logistics in Charge Loe Bendana or Dispatcher 
      LJ Ogoy reviews active driver schedules and bus statuses under the Logistics 
      Overview and assigns a bus and driver.
   3. [TRIP EXECUTION & BUDGET] -> Dispatcher LJ Ogoy reviews and dispatches the coach. 
      Petty cash budgets for tolls, fuel, and meals are requested and approved.
   4. [COMMISSIONS & COLLECTIONS] -> System auto-calculates driver commissions 
      which are approved for payroll. Customer collections are logged under Operations 
      Collections to settle outstanding invoicing.

MODULE I: ADMINISTRATION & DYNAMIC ACCESS MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Personnel: CEO Jhune Ernest R. Ogoy II, Administrator

Objective: Configure user accounts, track audit logs, and dynamically configure 
           role-level page visibility.

Step-by-Step Process:
1. Navigate to ADMIN -> USERS to register new personnel. Choose their specific 
   role (e.g. executive_vice_president, logistics_in_charge, service_adviser).
2. Go to ADMIN -> ROLE PERMISSIONS.
3. Select the target role using the premium segmented control bar.
4. Customize page visibility:
   - Click the Arrow (▶) next to a parent module to expand its sub-pages.
   - Toggle the checkboxes (View, Create, Edit, Delete). 
     * View toggle: Instantly manages whether the sidebar link is visible to that role.
     * Create/Edit/Delete: Toggles backend API capabilities.
5. Use "Grant All" or "Revoke All" for bulk configuration.
6. Click "Save Configuration" on the floating glassmorphic tray to apply changes.
7. Navigate to ADMIN -> AUDIT LOGS to review real-time database modifications.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE II: PROCUREMENT, PURCHASING & PMS APPROVALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Personnel: Service Adviser Minda Hernandez, Purchasing Manager Jaymart Ogoy, 
                   Operations Manager Rhazel Perez

Objective: Oversee Work Orders, generate Job Orders, issue P.O.s, and 
           manage vendor relationships.

Step-by-Step Process:
1. Navigate to PROCUREMENT -> SUPPLIERS. Click "Add Supplier" to record new vendors.
2. Go to ACCREDITATIONS. Operations Manager Rhazel Perez reviews and scores 
   vendors. She sets their status to "Accredited" to unlock purchasing.
3. WORK ORDER APPROVAL (Minda Hernandez):
   - Service Adviser Minda navigates to PROCUREMENT -> WORK ORDERS.
   - Reviews pending W.O.s requested by Head Mechanic Arnold or auto-generated by the PMS.
   - Click "Approve". This changes the status to "open" and automatically 
     generates a customizable Job Order (J.O.).
4. JOB ORDER PROCESSING (Jaymart Ogoy & Minda Hernandez):
   - Go to JOB ORDERS. Review the J.O.
   - If parts must be purchased: Click "Generate P.O." and choose an Accredited Supplier.
   - Build the purchase line items. The system automatically computes unit totals.
   - Click "Submit Purchase Order".
   - If no parts are required, bypass the P.O. stage, execute the repair, and click 
     "Complete Job Order".
5. VIEW BILLING & REPORTS: Purchasing Manager Jaymart and Service Adviser Minda 
   navigate to ACCOUNTING -> BILLING to view invoices fully tailored to show 
   procurement costs and supplier receivables.

MODULE III: OPERATIONS & TRIP TICKETING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Personnel: Operations Manager Rhazel Perez, Reservation Officer Mia Abella, 
                   Corporate Secretary Crisalyn Cahalhal

Objective: Create customer charters, process petty cash, and manage collections.

Step-by-Step Process:
1. Navigate to OPERATIONS -> TRIP TICKETS. Click "Create Trip Ticket".
2. Set Trip Type: "Domestic" (teal badge) or "International" (violet badge). 
   Select the booking customer, route, bus category, and timing.
3. Click "Submit". The ticket is immediately queued in Logistics for allocation.
4. Go to CASH BUDGETS. Operations Manager Rhazel Perez reviews petty cash requests 
   submitted for dispatches. Click "Approve Request" to authorize payout.
5. Go to COMMISSIONS. Select a completed trip, verify calculated fees, and click 
   "Approve Commission" to push the payout to HR payroll records.
6. Go to COLLECTIONS to view outstanding charter accounts. Click "Record 
   Collection" when customer check or cash payments are received.

MODULE V: INVENTORY, SUPPLIES & PMS AUTO-TRIGGERS
================================================================================
Primary Personnel: EVP Rhean Umali, Service Adviser Minda Hernandez, 
                   Head Mechanic Arnold Navero, Logistics in Charge Loe Bendana

Objective: Manage spare parts stock, track bus registrations, and oversee PMS.

Step-by-Step Process:
1. Go to INVENTORY -> SUPPLIES to log spare parts (tires, belts, oil). 
   EVP Rhean Umali and Service Adviser Minda Hernandez review inventory quantities 
   regularly.
2. Go to FLEET to track bus plate numbers, mileage logs, and registrations.
3. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SYSTEM (PMS) DIRECT LOOP:
   - When a bus records mileage exceeding 5,000 km since its last service (or 90 days), 
     the PMS automatically triggers a "Maintenance Alert".
   - The PMS automatically creates a new Work Order (`pending_approval`) and flags the 
     bus status as `under_maintenance` in the registry.
   - Service Adviser Minda Hernandez is notified to approve the Work Order.
   - Head Mechanic Arnold Navero assigns technicians to perform PMS procedures.
   - Once complete and the J.O. is closed, the PMS automatically resets the bus 
     mileage and days, updating the bus status back to `available`.

3. GENERAL SYSTEM RULES & RE-LOGIN POLICIES
================================================================================

- RE-LOGIN / SESSION REFRESH POLICY:
  When an Administrator adjusts role page permissions in ADMIN -> ROLE PERMISSIONS, 
  impacted employees must RE-LOGIN or manually refresh their browser window. The 
  frontend loads user permissions into the active session cache during authentication.
  
- AUTOMATION PIPELINE ENFORCEMENT:
  Avoid creating isolated Job Orders or Purchase Orders from scratch. Always initiate 
  requests from the source Work Order or Job Order to maintain strict audit trails 
  and prevent redundant double entries.

- ACCREDITATION REQUIREMENT:
  Purchase Orders can only be assigned to vendors qualified under the Supplier 
  Accreditation sub-module. Unaccredited suppliers will not appear in the PO 
  vendor selection lists.
